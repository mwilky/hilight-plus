[B]HiLight Plus[/B] unleashes the hardware potential of the eight-LED rear ring on the Google Pixel 11 Pro series.

While stock Pixel software limits the ring to Gemini interactions and basic calls from favourites, HiLight Plus takes over the ring to provide full customization: granular per-contact or per-app patterns, battery telemetry indicators, split-ring alert arcs, and extensive scheduling conditions.

Gemini's stock lighting states remain untouched. All processing executes locally on-device—no accounts, no tracking, and zero telemetry.

[HR][/HR]

[SIZE=5][B]Features[/B][/SIZE]

[B]Incoming Calls[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Dedicated pattern and hex/preset colour per contact.
[*]Configurable fallback defaults for other saved contacts and unknown callers.
[*]Favourites style for starred contacts, used when a caller has no rule of their own.
[*]Universal support: works across cellular calls and VoIP (WhatsApp, Teams, Google Meet, etc.).
[*]Active render loop stays lit until the call is answered or rejected.
[*]Optional missed-call style that stays lit until the missed-call notification is dismissed.
[/LIST]

[B]Notifications[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Per-sender and per-app rules with dynamic colour extraction from app icons.
[*]Starred contacts can share a favourites style, sitting between a sender's own rule and the app rule.
[*]13 distinct LED animation patterns: [I]Solid, Breathe, Pulse, Wave, Comet, Orbit, Beacon, Ripple, Sparkle, Rainbow[/I], plus copies of Gemini's own [I]Listening, Thinking[/I] and [I]Replying[/I] effects.
[*]Multi-alert ring management:
[LIST]
[*][B]Newest only:[/B] Displays the most recent notification for a set duration.
[*][B]Take turns:[/B] Sequentially cycles through active notifications until dismissed.
[*][B]Split the ring:[/B] Partitions the 8-LED ring into distinct coloured arcs (up to 4 notifications concurrently). Arcs can breathe, stay solid, brighten in turn (Spotlight) or circle the ring (Rotate).
[/LIST]
[*]Instant cleanup: lights turn off the moment the notification is cleared from the shade.
[/LIST]

[B]Battery & Charging Status[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Live charging gauge, fill animations, or gradient rings when connected to power.
[*]Dynamic red-to-green gradient mapped to charge percentage, or static user colours.
[*]Full-charge indicator with an auto-off sleep timer.
[*]Customizable low-battery alert threshold.
[*]Configurable layer priority (can sit below alerts or override standard notifications).
[/LIST]

[B]Gating & Environment Conditions[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Face-down only mode (accelerometer engages only when alerts are pending).
[*]Automatic Do Not Disturb (DND) suppression.
[*]Global quiet hours scheduler with per-rule override options.
[/LIST]

[B]Extras[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Live pattern preview on a ring mock-up, and a Test on LEDs button in every rule editor.
[*]Guided onboarding that detects each setting as you flip it, with a notification that follows you into Settings to take the pairing code.
[*]Live debug log for the app and daemon, with one-tap share (contact names left out).
[*]Reset lights button that frees a stuck ring without a reboot, plus an automatic check on unlock.
[/LIST]

[HR][/HR]

[SIZE=5][B]Requirements[/B][/SIZE]

[LIST=1]
[*][B]Google Pixel 11 Pro / Pro XL[/B] running [B]Android 17 (API 37)[/B].
[*][B]Developer options and Wireless debugging[/B] for setup. No root or Shizuku needed: HiLight Plus pairs once with the phone's own Wireless debugging to start a small shell-privileged daemon that reaches the lights service, then reconnects by itself, including after a reboot.
[*][B]To keep working[/B], Developer options must stay on along with at least one of USB debugging or Wireless debugging. Wi-Fi is needed whenever the daemon has to start (setup, reboot, update), as Wireless debugging only runs on Wi-Fi.
[*][B][URL='https://shizuku.rikka.app/']Shizuku[/URL][/B] (optional) is still supported, via Wireless Debugging or Root, for anyone who already uses it.
[*][B]Stock "Calls from favourites" disabled[/B] in system settings to prevent hardware race conditions. Onboarding verifies this setup automatically.
[/LIST]
